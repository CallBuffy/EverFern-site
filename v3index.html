Talk With Us About Your Eye Practice

Retina drug economics

High-cost injectable drugs are often a retina practice's largest expense and its largest revenue line. We manage both sides, from acquisition cost and inventory to authorizations, billing and collections, so every dose is paid for and margin isn't lost between purchase and payment.

Prior authorizations and drug coverage rules

Payer rules for injectables change constantly, including requirements to try a lower-cost drug first. We keep authorizations current and stop preventable denials before they happen.

Surgery center and premium services

ASC operations, surgical scheduling and patient-pay options such as premium lenses, with clear financial policies and accurate collections.

Management company transitions

Changing billing or management companies is high risk for an eye practice. We plan and run the transition so cash keeps moving.

"Retina practices live and die on drug margins and authorizations. We manage them like an owner would, because we have run an eye practice ourselves."

Our team

Buffy Howard, PhD

Founder & CEO

Buffy has spent 25 years leading healthcare organizations through growth, crisis and change. She has served as CEO of a multi-specialty ophthalmology practice and surgery center, COO of a five-clinic radiation oncology practice, Regional Vice President of a multi-state urgent care and workers' compensation platform, and Vice President of Innovative Payer Models at an ACO. Her career began in long-term care, where she spent fifteen years turning around struggling facilities and earned a national Baldrige quality award. She also owned and operated a licensed adult family home. Buffy holds a PhD in Leadership Studies and an MBA, teaches health care policy in St. Martin's University's MBA program, and served as a governor-appointed chapter governor of the Washington Health Care Association.

Monica Tek

Director of Operations & Revenue Cycle
